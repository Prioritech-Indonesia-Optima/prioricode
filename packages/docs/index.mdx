PrioriCode is the open source AI coding agent built by [Prioritech Indonesia Optima](https://prioritech.co.id). It lives in your terminal, reads your codebase, runs your tools, and ships your code — one conversation at a time.

  <Card title="Terminal-native" icon="terminal" href="/quickstart">

## Install

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="macOS & Linux">```bash curl -fsSL https://github.com/Prioritech-Indonesia-Optima/prioricode/raw/main/install | bash ```</Tab>
  <Tab title="Windows">```powershell irm https://github.com/Prioritech-Indonesia-Optima/prioricode/raw/main/install.ps1 | iex ```</Tab>
</Tabs>

Or grab a binary from the [releases page](https://github.com/Prioritech-Indonesia-Optima/prioricode/releases). Ready to go? Head to the [quickstart](/quickstart).
